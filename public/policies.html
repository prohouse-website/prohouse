GUEST TERMS & CONDITIONS OF STAY (NSW)

To maintain a safe, comfortable, and compliant environment for all guests, residents, and neighbours, the following Terms & Conditions apply to all stays at ProHouse properties in Sydney, New South Wales.

By making a booking and/or staying at the property, you agree to these Terms & Conditions.

1. ID VERIFICATION (MANDATORY)

To confirm your booking, you must complete guest identity verification within 24 hours of request.

This includes:

• A valid passport or driver licence

• Completion of verification via ProHouse’s authorised verification partner (e.g. ChargeAutomation or equivalent)

This process is required to confirm guest identity, comply with applicable requirements, protect the property and residents, and prevent fraud and misuse.

As ProHouse properties do not have on-site reception, verification must be completed before arrival. Failure to do so may result in cancellation without refund.

2. RATES & PRICING

• All rates and availability are subject to change based on supply and demand and peak/off-peak periods.

• All bookings are subject to owner and/or strata approval.

• Prices are quoted in Australian Dollars (AUD) and include accommodation only unless stated otherwise.

• Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method. Processing may take 5–14 business days depending on the bank or payment provider.

• No refund will be provided in the event of a no-show.

• ProHouse is not liable for cancellations or disruptions caused by events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, government restrictions or emergencies. Guests are strongly advised to purchase comprehensive travel insurance.

• Card payment surcharge is up to 2%.

2.1 Flexible Booking

Payment Policy: A 50% deposit is required at booking. Final payment must be made at least 7 days prior to arrival.

Cancellation Policy: Guests may cancel at least 14 days prior to arrival and receive a full refund, less third-party bank, card or payment processing fees.

Booking Changes: Date changes are subject to availability and rate differences. Approved changes may incur an administration fee. New dates will be charged at the prevailing rate.

2.2 Non-Refundable Booking

Payment Policy: Full payment (100%) is required at booking.

Cancellation Policy: For direct ProHouse bookings, if you cancel in advance and ProHouse is able to resell the accommodation for the same dates, you may be eligible for a refund, subject to a 10% administration fee and applicable bank or transaction fees. Refunds under this clause are at ProHouse’s discretion and depend on successful rebooking.

Booking Changes: No changes are permitted.

3. PAYMENTS & ACCEPTANCE OF TERMS

• Payment of any deposit or booking fee constitutes acceptance of these Terms & Conditions.

• Payments must be made by credit card (surcharge up to 2%) or bank transfer. Funds must be received in our bank account to be considered paid; a transfer or payment receipt alone is not proof of cleared funds.

• ProHouse does not store credit card details; payments are processed through secure third-party providers such as Stripe or Square.

4. SECURITY BOND

• A security bond from $200 per room is required before check-in.

• The bond may be held as a pre-authorisation or cash bond, depending on booking type.

• The bond may be used to cover damage/breakages, missing items, excessive cleaning, smoking, noise or strata breaches, unauthorised guests/parties, late check-out, and costs incurred dealing with strata management, neighbours or authorities.

You authorise ProHouse to deduct applicable charges from the bond or payment method provided. If costs exceed the bond, you agree to pay the balance on demand.

Bond refunds are typically processed within 5–14 business days, subject to inspection and resolution of claims.

5. OCCUPANCY & USE OF PROPERTY

• Guest numbers must not exceed the confirmed booking.

• Visitors are not permitted without prior written approval.

• Parties, gatherings and events are strictly prohibited.

• The property may only be used for short-term residential accommodation.

Unauthorised occupancy may result in immediate termination without refund.

6. STRATA SCHEME & NSW CODE OF CONDUCT

Many ProHouse properties are in strata buildings. Guests must comply with all strata by-laws, building/security rules, and the NSW Short-Term Rental Accommodation Code of Conduct.

Breaches may result in termination of the stay, bond claims, and/or reporting to strata management or NSW Fair Trading as applicable.

7. NOISE & BEHAVIOUR

• Guests must respect neighbours at all times.

• Quiet hours: 10:00pm–7:00am.

• Noise must not be audible from neighbouring apartments or common property.

• Complaints from neighbours, strata, building management or authorities may result in termination of the stay.

ProHouse operates a strict no-party policy.

8. SMOKING, VAPING & ILLEGAL ACTIVITY

• Smoking and vaping are not permitted indoors.

• Evidence of smoking may result in additional cleaning and deodorising charges.

• Illegal activity is prohibited and may be reported to authorities where required.

9. PETS

• Pets are only permitted where expressly approved in writing.

• Pets must be disclosed at booking.

• Unapproved pets may result in termination of the stay.

• Guests are responsible for pet-related damage, cleaning and disturbance.

10. CHECK-IN & CHECK-OUT

• Procedures will be provided prior to arrival.

• Failure to follow procedures may cause delays or loss of entry.

• Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may incur fees.

11. DAMAGE, LOSS & CLEANING

• Guests must leave the property in the condition it was found, reasonable wear and tear excepted.

• Excess cleaning, rubbish removal, damage or missing items will be charged.

• Breakages or damage must be reported immediately.

• Lost keys, access devices or remotes will be replaced at the guest’s expense.

12. UTILITIES

Reasonable utility use is included. Excessive use (more than a 30% increase compared with similar periods) may be charged against the security deposit. Evidence of charges will be provided on request.

13. COMPLAINTS & MAINTENANCE

Issues must be reported as soon as they arise. Issues not reported during the stay may be difficult or impossible to investigate after departure. Photos are encouraged to assist resolution.

14. LIABILITY & INSURANCE

ProHouse and property owners are not responsible for loss, theft or damage to personal belongings, subject to applicable law. Guests are strongly encouraged to obtain travel insurance.

15. TERMINATION

ProHouse may terminate a stay where these Terms & Conditions or strata by-laws are materially breached, payment obligations are not met, or guest behaviour impacts safety or amenity, subject to applicable law.

16. FLEXIBLE CANCELLATION

Direct bookings may offer more flexible cancellation terms, including selected non-refundable promotions where expressly stated in the booking conditions. The terms shown for the selected rate at booking prevail.

17. DISCLAIMER

ProHouse acts as an accommodation provider on behalf of property owners. Nothing in these Terms & Conditions excludes, restricts or modifies any consumer guarantee, right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

18. GOVERNING LAW

These Terms & Conditions are governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia. Any dispute is subject to the jurisdiction of the courts and tribunals having jurisdiction in New South Wales.

Complaint Policy

Purpose and Scope

This policy outlines ProHouse’s commitment to handling complaints fairly, efficiently, and professionally. Our goals are to:

  • Recognise and protect our guests’ and clients’ right to raise complaints.
  • Provide a clear, accessible, and well-communicated complaints process.
  • Ensure fairness to both the complainant and any staff or parties involved.
  • Respond to complaints promptly, courteously, and transparently.
  • Identify remedies and solutions where appropriate.
  • Record, assess, and review complaints regularly to improve our services.

How to Make a Complaint

Complaints can be made in several ways:

  • In writing: via email or postal mail.
  • Verbally: in person or by phone (follow-up in writing is appreciated).
  • Feedback forms or surveys: through our website or onsite forms.

Anyone can make a complaint, including guests, service providers, corporate clients, or community organisations.

Steps for Making a Complaint

While staying at ProHouse
Many issues can be resolved quickly by speaking directly to our staff. Staff aim to address and resolve problems within 24 hours wherever possible. Please notify our team as soon as a problem arises so we can act promptly.

Other complaints
You can contact the ProHouse office directly and provide your complaint in writing. The sooner we know about the issue, the sooner we can work on a resolution.

Provide clear details
When making a complaint, please include as much information as possible, such as what happened, when it happened, supporting documents/photos/evidence, and how you think the issue can be resolved.

How ProHouse Handles Complaints

  • Listen carefully to your concerns.
  • Gather relevant information and speak with all parties involved.
  • Provide answers to your questions wherever possible.
  • Take professional steps to resolve the issue promptly.
  • Keep you updated on the progress of your complaint.
  • Strive to achieve a fair and mutually satisfactory outcome.

At ProHouse, we view complaints as an opportunity to improve our services and help ensure every guest has a comfortable and enjoyable stay.

Customer Service Commitment

At ProHouse, we are dedicated to providing an exceptional guest experience. Our commitments include:

  • Delivering courteous, responsive, respectful, and knowledgeable service at all times.
  • Offering the highest possible level of service, within the resources available.
  • Providing accurate information and recommendations to enhance your experience of Sydney and surrounding areas.
  • Ensuring that all property information and descriptions are honest, clear, and kept up to date.
  • Making it easy for guests to submit feedback or suggestions, and regularly seeking your opinions to improve our services.
  • Training our staff to maintain consistent, high-quality customer service.
  • Providing the same level of service to all guests, regardless of age, race, gender, nationality, background, or any other factor.

At ProHouse, our goal is to make every stay comfortable, convenient, and enjoyable.

Privacy Statement

Last updated: 1 September 2026

ProHouse respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently. This Privacy Statement explains how ProHouse collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information when you make an enquiry, make a booking, stay at a ProHouse property, use our website, or otherwise interact with us.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on your interaction with ProHouse, we may collect information including:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number and residential address.
  • Booking and accommodation details, including arrival and departure dates and guest numbers.
  • Passport, driver licence or other identification information required for guest verification.
  • Information supplied during our pre-arrival and identity-verification processes.
  • Payment and transaction information.
  • Security bond and credit-card pre-authorisation information.
  • Communications between you and ProHouse.
  • Complaints, feedback and customer-service records.
  • Information relating to damage, incidents, building access, security or breaches of property rules.
  • Vehicle and parking information where parking is provided.
  • Technical information associated with use of our website or online services, such as IP address, browser/device information and website activity where collected by our service providers.

We seek to collect information reasonably required to manage bookings, provide accommodation and related services, protect our properties and guests, comply with applicable requirements and operate our business.

2. Identity Verification

ProHouse accommodation may use self check-in and may not have an on-site reception. For security, fraud prevention and accommodation-management purposes, guests may therefore be required to complete identity verification before arrival.

This may involve providing a passport, driver licence or other acceptable identification through ProHouse or an authorised third-party verification provider. Where a third-party service is used, information submitted through that service may also be handled in accordance with that provider's privacy policy and security practices.

3. Payment Information

Payments, security deposits and credit-card pre-authorisations may be processed using third-party payment providers such as Stripe, Square or other payment platforms used by ProHouse. ProHouse does not intentionally store complete credit-card numbers on its own website. Payment providers may independently collect and process payment information in accordance with their own privacy policies and security requirements.

4. How We Use Personal Information

  • Process and manage accommodation bookings.
  • Verify guest identity.
  • Communicate with you before, during and after your stay.
  • Provide check-in and check-out information.
  • Process payments, refunds, security bonds and authorised charges.
  • Provide customer service and respond to enquiries.
  • Manage housekeeping, maintenance and property access.
  • Investigate complaints, incidents, damage or alleged breaches.
  • Prevent fraud, unauthorised bookings and misuse of accommodation.
  • Manage safety and security.
  • Communicate with property owners, strata/building management or service providers where reasonably necessary.
  • Comply with applicable laws, lawful requests and regulatory requirements.
  • Maintain business and accounting records.
  • Improve ProHouse's properties, services and guest experience.
  • Protect the legitimate interests, property and legal rights of ProHouse, property owners, guests and other affected parties.

5. When Information May Be Shared

ProHouse does not sell guests' personal information. We may disclose relevant personal information where reasonably necessary to organisations or persons involved in providing or administering your accommodation, including property owners, booking platforms and channel partners, property-management and booking software providers, identity-verification providers, payment processors, housekeeping and maintenance providers, building or strata management, security providers, IT/hosting/communications providers, professional advisers, and government agencies or authorities where required or permitted by law.

6. Booking Platforms and Third-Party Services

If you book through a third-party platform, that platform may independently collect and process your information under its own privacy policy. ProHouse may receive booking information from these platforms to administer your reservation. We may also use third-party systems for property management, guest communications, payment processing, identity verification, security deposits and website hosting.

7. Overseas Service Providers

Some technology, booking, payment, hosting or communications providers used by ProHouse may store or process information outside Australia. The countries involved may vary according to the service provider and its infrastructure. Where practicable, ProHouse selects established service providers and takes reasonable steps appropriate to the circumstances to protect personal information handled through those services.

8. Security and CCTV

Some ProHouse properties or the buildings in which they are located may have CCTV or other security systems in entrances, lifts, car parks and common areas. Such systems may be operated by the building, strata management, security providers or, where applicable, ProHouse. CCTV is not intended to be installed in private areas such as bedrooms or bathrooms.

9. Data Security

ProHouse takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Retention of Information

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably required for the purposes for which it was collected, including booking administration, accounting, dispute management, fraud prevention and legal or regulatory requirements. Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted, destroyed or de-identified where appropriate and subject to applicable record-keeping obligations.

11. Website, Cookies and Analytics

The ProHouse website and third-party services connected to it may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, security, analytics and performance. You may be able to restrict cookies through your browser settings, although doing so may affect certain website functions.

12. Marketing Communications

Where permitted, ProHouse may use contact information to communicate relevant accommodation information, offers or updates. You may ask us to stop sending marketing communications at any time. Operational communications relating to an existing booking, payment, property access, security or your stay are not considered marketing communications.

13. Accessing or Correcting Your Information

You may contact ProHouse to request access to personal information we hold about you or to ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, out of date or incomplete. We may need to verify your identity before responding. In some circumstances, access may be restricted where permitted or required by law.

14. Privacy Complaints

If you believe ProHouse has not handled your personal information appropriately, please contact us and provide details of your concern. We will review the matter and aim to respond within a reasonable period. You may also have the right to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) regarding privacy matters.

15. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to booking platforms, payment providers or other third-party websites. ProHouse is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. You should review the relevant provider's privacy policy before providing personal information.

16. Changes to This Privacy Statement

ProHouse may update this Privacy Statement from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, business practices or applicable requirements. The current version will be published on the ProHouse website together with its most recent update date.
