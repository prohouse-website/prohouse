BEFORE YOU BOOK

Sydney Airport stay questions.

Is ProHouse inside Sydney Airport?

ProHouse’s airport property is in Wolli Creek, near Sydney Airport. It is outside the terminals. Arrange transport to the correct terminal for your flight.

Am I booking a private room or the whole apartment?

A private-room booking gives you a private bedroom in a shared apartment. Kitchen and living spaces are shared. A whole-apartment booking gives you exclusive use of the space described in that listing. Always check the selected listing.

Can I stay before an early flight or after a late arrival?

Check availability for your dates and complete arrival preparation in advance. Self check-in is available after the required approval and release of your access instructions. Approval hours are Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm; there is no onsite reception.

Can I visit Sydney CBD from Wolli Creek?

Yes. Plan a rail journey from Wolli Creek Station to central Sydney. Check current services, walking routes and connections using the Transport for NSW trip planner.

How do I check the total price?

Choose your dates and guest count on the booking page to see availability, rates and booking conditions. Contact ProHouse if you need help choosing a stay.
