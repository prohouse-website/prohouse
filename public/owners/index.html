A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before you
hand us the keys.

What does “from 10% commission” mean?

Our management commission starts at 10%. Your agreed rate and the services included depend on your property and the management plan. We’ll explain the commission basis, platform fees and other costs before you sign.

Does my home need to be furnished?

Tell us what’s already in place. We can help you plan the furnishings and essentials needed for guests. The first-eight-owner offer includes complimentary personal shopping support; purchases are paid by you.

Can my home really be ready in three days?

A suitable home that needs minimal preparation can be ready in as little as three days. Photography, access, furniture deliveries and any required work can affect the timeline. We’ll confirm your launch plan after reviewing the home.

How do you manage prices and bookings?

We use a PMS and automation to coordinate bookings and guest communication, with PriceLabs helping us manage competitive rates as market conditions change.
