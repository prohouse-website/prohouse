Private room or whole apartment for your Sydney short stay?

By ProHouse · Published 6 November 2026

Two listings can show a similar bedroom photograph while offering very different stays. Before comparing prices, establish whether you are booking one room or exclusive use of an apartment. That choice affects privacy, shared facilities and how comfortably the space fits your trip.

Start with what is exclusively yours

A private-room booking gives you the bedroom described in the listing. Other spaces may be shared with other guests. A whole-apartment booking gives you exclusive use of the apartment described in that particular listing; it does not mean exclusive use of the building or its common facilities.

At ProHouse’s Wolli Creek shared apartment, private-room guests have their own bedroom and ensuite, while the kitchen and living room are shared. Check the Room 1, Room 2 and Room 3 pages and your selected booking details for the exact room.

Compare the bathroom as carefully as the bed

“Private room” alone does not tell you whether the bathroom is shared, dedicated or directly connected to the bedroom. Look for the bathroom description, room photographs and any access arrangements. If the wording is unclear, ask before booking.

For a group, also check the number of bathrooms and sleeping arrangements. A room’s capacity is the capacity of that booking, not permission to use the other bedrooms in a shared apartment.

Think about how you will use the shared areas

A shared kitchen can suit a guest who wants to prepare breakfast or a simple meal. It also requires consideration: clear up after cooking, leave space for others and follow the property’s quiet-hours rules. A shared living room is available to other guests too.

If your trip depends on having a lounge, kitchen and several bedrooms entirely to your group, compare whole-apartment options rather than assuming those areas come exclusively with a private room.

Compare the total for the same trip

Use the same dates, guest count and length of stay for each option. Check the final price, cleaning charges, parking arrangements and cancellation conditions. A security pre-authorisation, where required, should be understood separately from the accommodation price.

For a short airport stopover, the arrival process may matter as much as the room. A self check-in property still requires you to complete its preparation steps. Read our self check-in checklist before travelling.

A quick decision checklist

  • Is the booking for a room or the whole apartment?
  • Is the bathroom an ensuite, dedicated bathroom or shared bathroom?
  • Which kitchen and living areas are shared?
  • Does the exact bed setup suit every booked guest?
  • Can you complete the arrival requirements before your trip?
  • Have you compared the final price and cancellation conditions?

Choose the space that fits how you will actually stay. You can compare ProHouse stays and then check availability for your dates.
