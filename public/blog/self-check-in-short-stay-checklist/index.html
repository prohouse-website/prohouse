A self check-in checklist for your Sydney short stay

By ProHouse · Published 6 November 2026

Self check-in gives you a way to arrive without collecting a key at a reception desk. A smooth arrival begins before you travel: finish the required checks, understand when access becomes available and keep the correct instructions close at hand.

In this guide

  2. Before travelling: finish the required steps
  3. Save the link, not a guessed door code
  4. Check your phone and your parking plan
  5. If you need help on arrival

Before booking: check whether the arrival process suits your trip

Read the property’s check-in time, approval hours and support arrangements. An accommodation business can offer self check-in while having limited staff support outside office hours. Do not assume there is an onsite reception or that an unfinished verification can be approved late at night.

ProHouse uses self check-in with no onsite reception. Arrival approvals take place Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm. If you are travelling outside that window, complete the required preparation in advance. Check the airport accommodation page and the instructions sent for your booking.

Before travelling: finish the required steps

Find your pre-arrival email and use its link to complete identity verification, provide your estimated arrival time and finish the required security-deposit steps. Completing a form and receiving approval are different stages; check the status shown for your own booking.

Use the contact details on the official ProHouse website if something is unclear. Avoid putting identity documents or payment details into an unfamiliar link sent by someone claiming to represent the property.

Save the link, not a guessed door code

Keep the same arrival link available on your phone. For ProHouse, access details are released through that link from 3pm on arrival day after the required steps are completed. An empty access section before release time does not necessarily mean something has gone wrong.

Read the full route from the street entrance to your booked room. Building access, a lockbox and a room lock may be separate steps. A map pin or building name alone may not identify the correct guest entrance.

Check your phone and your parking plan

  • Charge your phone and keep a way to access your arrival email.
  • Confirm the exact booked address and room.
  • Check the arrival time and when your access details become available.
  • Arrange any required parking before relying on it.
  • Save the property’s official contact information.
  • Read the instructions before you reach the entrance.

Keep access links and codes private. Do not share them publicly or use another guest’s instructions. Parking arrangements are separate from bedroom access; follow the information provided for your own booking.

If you need help on arrival

Check that you are using the right entrance, your own booking link and the instructions for the current stay. Contact the property with a clear description of the step that is not working. Do not send a door code in a public message or review.

ProHouse support outside business hours is limited. Follow the emergency and contact instructions in your arrival information rather than assuming immediate assistance is available. If you have not booked yet, ask about the arrival process before choosing your dates.
