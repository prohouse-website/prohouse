YOUR QUESTIONS. ANSWERED.

Everything you need to know before listing your home with ProHouse.

Have another question? Ask us ↗
How much does ProHouse management cost?

Our management commission starts at 10%. Your final rate, the income it applies to and the included services are agreed in your management plan before you sign.

What is included in the free $2,000 onboarding package?

The first eight owners to join receive a complimentary onboarding package valued at $2,000, including photography, personal shopping support and a preparation and listing plan. We confirm availability and the package scope with you before onboarding.

Are there any upfront or ongoing costs?

The first-eight-owner offer covers the agreed onboarding package. Furniture, household purchases, cleaning, repairs and other property costs are discussed separately. We’ll set out management commission, any platform fees and the costs that apply to your home before you commit.

Is the furniture included with the personal shopper?

The personal shopping service is complimentary under the first-eight-owner offer. You pay for the furniture and products you choose. We work with your budget and what your home already has.

Where will you advertise my home?

We create and manage listings on popular booking platforms including Airbnb, Booking.com and Expedia, with clear descriptions and photography to help guests choose your home.

How do you keep my rates competitive?

We use PriceLabs to help manage rates around market demand and seasonality. Our PMS coordinates bookings and calendars, while automation helps keep guest communication and arrival information consistent.

Who looks after guests and my home?

ProHouse manages guest communication and coordinates housekeeping and maintenance as part of your agreed management plan. We’ll explain the services, responsibilities and contact arrangements before you hand over the keys.

Can my home be guest-ready in three days?

A suitable home needing minimal preparation can be ready in as little as three days. Photography, access, deliveries and any work needed can affect the timing. We agree your launch date after reviewing the property.

How and when will I receive my rental income?

Your payout arrangements, statement schedule and deductions are set out in your management agreement. We’ll talk you through them before you join, so you know how your income and costs will be handled.
