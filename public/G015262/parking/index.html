Paid Parking

Terms & conditions

  • Book and pay in advance. Contact your host before arrival to reserve parking and arrange the fob.
  • Use only your assigned space 1.
  • Return the fob to your bedside table before departure.
  • Follow posted signs, strata by-laws and building rules.
  • Keep driveways, accessways, fire exits and emergency access clear.
  • Unauthorised or improperly parked vehicles may be reported to building management and may be subject to enforcement where legally permitted.
  • The vehicle owner is responsible for any damage caused by their vehicle.

Follow these steps

  1. 01
    Use the loading zone

    Park temporarily in the loading zone beside Unit G01.

  2. 02
    Collect the fob

    The parking fob will be on the bedside table inside your room.

  3. 03
    Drive to the reserved space

    After the roller gate, drive to the end of the road and make three left turns.

  4. 04
    Park in space 1

    Your reserved parking space is clearly marked 1.

  5. 05
    Return via Lift A

    The nearest lift is on your left. Take the lift to level G.

  6. 06
    When leaving

    Use the fob to re-enter the building and lift, take the lift to level -1, then drive out of the car park.

  7. 07
    Return the fob

    Place it back on your bedside table, then lock your room door and the apartment entrance door.
