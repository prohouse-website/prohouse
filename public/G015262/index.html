Step by step

Entering and leaving the visitor car park

1

Arrive at the garage

You will need a second person inside the apartment to unlock the garage roller door.

2

Request entry

At the intercom, use the arrow to find G01, then press the bell button.

3

Unlock from inside

The person inside the apartment should press the key button on the apartment intercom.

4

Use a visitor bay

Park only in a bay clearly marked VISITOR.

5

Return to ground floor

The nearest lift is on your left. Take Lift A to level G.

6

Leave the car park

Drive up towards the exit. The roller door will open automatically as your vehicle approaches.

03

Reserved parking option

Pay parking

Advance booking required

Contact your host before arrival to purchase parking and arrange access to the parking fob.

  1. 01
    Use the loading zone

    Park temporarily in the loading zone beside Unit G01.

  2. 02
    Collect the fob

    The parking fob will be on the bedside table inside your room.

  3. 03
    Drive to the reserved space

    After the roller gate, drive to the end of the road and make three left turns.

  4. 04
    Park in space 1

    Your reserved parking space is clearly marked 1.

  5. 05
    Return via Lift A

    The nearest lift is on your left. Take the lift to level G.

  6. 06
    When leaving

    Use the fob to re-enter the building and lift, take the lift to level -1, then drive out of the car park.

  7. 07
    Return the fob

    Place it back on your bedside table, then lock your room door and the apartment entrance door.
