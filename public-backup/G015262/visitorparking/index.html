Step by step

Entering and leaving the visitor car park

1

Arrive at the garage

You will need a second person inside the apartment to unlock the garage roller door.

2

Request entry

At the intercom, use the arrow to find G01, then press the bell button.

3

Unlock from inside

The person inside the apartment should press the key button on the apartment intercom.

4

Use a visitor bay

Park only in a bay clearly marked VISITOR.

5

Return to ground floor

The nearest lift is on your left. Take Lift A to level G.

6

Leave the car park

Drive up towards the exit. The roller door will open automatically as your vehicle approaches.
